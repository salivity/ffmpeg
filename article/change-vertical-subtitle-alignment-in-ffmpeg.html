Change Vertical Subtitle Alignment in FFmpeg

Burning subtitles directly into a video using FFmpeg allows you to hardcode text so it appears permanently on any media player. You can easily adjust the vertical alignment—moving subtitles to the top, middle, or bottom of the frame, or fine-tuning their exact vertical offset—by using the force_style option within the subtitles video filter.

The Subtitle Alignment System

FFmpeg relies on the SubStation Alpha (SSA/ASS) style specification to position text. In this system, alignment is defined by numbers corresponding to a standard numeric keypad:

  • Top: 7 (Top-Left), 8 (Top-Center), 9 (Top-Right)
  • Middle: 4 (Middle-Left), 5 (Middle-Center), 6 (Middle-Right)
  • Bottom: 1 (Bottom-Left), 2 (Bottom-Center), 3 (Bottom-Right)

By default, standard SRT subtitles are rendered at the bottom center (Alignment=2).

Move Subtitles to the Top of the Video

To move the subtitles to the top-center of the screen, set Alignment=8 using the force_style parameter:

ffmpeg -i input.mp4 -vf "subtitles=subtitles.srt:force_style='Alignment=8'" -c:a copy output.mp4

Move Subtitles to the Middle of the Screen

To center the subtitles vertically and horizontally:

ffmpeg -i input.mp4 -vf "subtitles=subtitles.srt:force_style='Alignment=5'" -c:a copy output.mp4

Fine-Tuning Vertical Position with MarginV

If you want subtitles near the bottom or top but need to adjust the exact distance from the edge, use the MarginV setting. MarginV defines the vertical margin in pixels.

To keep subtitles at the bottom center (Alignment=2) but raise them 50 pixels higher than normal to avoid overlapping on-screen graphics:

ffmpeg -i input.mp4 -vf "subtitles=subtitles.srt:force_style='Alignment=2,MarginV=50'" -c:a copy output.mp4

If Alignment is set to the top (e.g., Alignment=8), MarginV dictates how far down the text appears from the top edge.

Combining Styles

You can combine alignment adjustments with other styling properties such as font size and color:

ffmpeg -i input.mp4 -vf "subtitles=subtitles.srt:force_style='Alignment=8,MarginV=30,FontSize=24,PrimaryColour=&H00FFFFFF&'" -c:a copy output.mp4

In this command, the subtitles are anchored to the top center with a 30-pixel margin, a font size of 24, and white text.
