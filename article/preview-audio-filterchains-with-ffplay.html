Preview Audio Filterchains with FFplay

Testing complex audio filters directly in ffmpeg can be time-consuming because it requires rendering, encoding, and saving a new file to disk before you can listen to the result. By using ffplay, the lightweight media player included with the FFmpeg suite, you can listen to audio filterchains rendered in real time. This guide explains how to apply the -af flag in ffplay to audition audio filters instantly before committing to a final ffmpeg render.

Basic Syntax

The syntax for applying audio filters in ffplay is identical to ffmpeg. You pass the -af (or -filter:a) option followed by your filter string.

To play an audio file with a filter while disabling the default video/visualizer display window, use the -nodisp flag:

ffplay -nodisp -af "filter_name=parameter1=value:parameter2=value" input.wav

If you omit -nodisp, ffplay opens an SDL window displaying an audio waveform or frequency spectrum, which can also be useful for visual monitoring.

Common Audio Preview Examples

1. Volume and Equalization

To audition a volume boost alongside a peaking equalizer filter:

ffplay -nodisp -af "volume=1.5,equalizer=f=1000:t=q:w=1:g=3" input.mp3

In this command:

  • volume=1.5 increases the overall amplitude by 50%.
  • equalizer=f=1000:t=q:w=1:g=3 adds a 3dB gain at 1000 Hz with a Q factor of 1.

2. Highpass and Lowpass Filtering

To preview band-limiting (removing low-end rumble and harsh high frequencies):

ffplay -nodisp -af "highpass=f=80,lowpass=f=12000" input.wav

3. Dynamic Range Compression

Previewing compressors is critical because incorrect attack, release, or threshold settings can cause audio distortion or unnatural pumping:

ffplay -nodisp -af "acompressor=threshold=-20dB:ratio=4:attack=5:release=50" input.wav

4. Loudness Normalization (EBU R128)

To test how dynamic normalization affects your track's balance:

ffplay -nodisp -af "loudnorm=I=-16:TP=-1.5:LRA=11" input.wav

Looping for Repetitive Auditioning

When fine-tuning parameters, it is helpful to loop a specific section of the audio file repeatedly:

ffplay -nodisp -ss 00:00:30 -t 10 -loop 0 -af "acompressor=threshold=-15dB:ratio=3" input.wav
  • -ss 00:00:30 starts playback at 30 seconds.
  • -t 10 limits playback duration to 10 seconds.
  • -loop 0 loops playback indefinitely until you close the player.

Migrating from FFplay to FFmpeg

Once the audio sounds correct in ffplay, copy the entire filterchain string into your ffmpeg command. Replace ffplay -nodisp with ffmpeg -i and define your output codecs and container:

Previewing with ffplay:

ffplay -nodisp -af "highpass=f=100,acompressor=threshold=-18dB:ratio=3,volume=1.2" input.wav

Encoding with ffmpeg:

ffmpeg -i input.wav -af "highpass=f=100,acompressor=threshold=-18dB:ratio=3,volume=1.2" -c:a aac -b:a 192k output.m4a

Using this workflow prevents unnecessary encoding passes, reduces disk I/O, and ensures your filter parameters are dialled in accurately before rendering large batches of files.
